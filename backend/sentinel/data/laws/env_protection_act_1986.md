# Environment (Protection) Act, 1986

## Section 2 — Definitions: Environment, Pollutant, Pollution
"Environment" includes water, air, land, and the inter-relationship between them and human beings and other living creatures. "Environmental pollutant" means any solid, liquid, or gaseous substance present in such concentration as may be injurious to the environment. "Environmental pollution" means presence of such a pollutant in the environment.

## Section 3 — Umbrella Powers of the Central Government
The Central Government has the power to take all measures it deems necessary or expedient for the purpose of protecting and improving the quality of the environment and preventing, controlling, and abating environmental pollution. This is the umbrella provision under which all subordinate environmental rules (e-waste, plastic waste, noise, solid waste, etc.) are enacted.

## Section 5 — Power to Issue Directions
The Central Government may issue written directions to any person, officer, or authority to comply with environmental norms, shut down or prohibit operations, or take remedial measures. The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) exercises this power.

## Section 7 — Prohibition on Discharge Beyond Standards
No person carrying on an industry, operation, or process shall discharge or emit or permit discharge or emission of any environmental pollutant in excess of standards prescribed under Schedule II or notified by the government.

## Section 15 — Penalties
Contravention of any provision, rule, order, or direction under this Act is punishable with imprisonment up to five years, or a fine up to one lakh rupees, or both. If the violation continues, an additional fine of up to five thousand rupees for each day of continuation is imposed. After one year of continued violation, imprisonment may extend to seven years.

## Section 16 — Offences by Companies
Where an offence is committed by a company, every person in charge of and responsible for the conduct of the business of the company at the time of the offence is deemed guilty unless they prove the offence was committed without their knowledge or they exercised due diligence.

## Section 17 — Authority: MoEFCC and Delegated Boards
The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) is the apex authority. Enforcement is delegated to the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards (KSPCB in Karnataka). District collectors, police, and ULBs may be co-opted to enforce specific provisions.
