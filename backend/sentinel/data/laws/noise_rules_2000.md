# Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000

## Rule 3 — Ambient Noise Standards by Zone
Permissible noise levels (Leq in dB(A)) by land-use zone:
- Industrial Area: Day 75, Night 70
- Commercial Area: Day 65, Night 55
- Residential Area: Day 55, Night 45
- Silence Zone (hospitals, schools, courts): Day 50, Night 40
Day time is 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM; night time is 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

## Rule 3(3) — Silence Zones
Areas within 100 metres of the boundary of schools, colleges, hospitals, and courts are declared silence zones. No loudspeakers, public address systems, or horns shall be used in these areas. Vehicles must turn off horns in silence zones.

## Rule 4 — Restrictions on Loudspeakers and Sound Amplifiers
A loudspeaker or public address system shall not be used at night (10 PM to 6 AM) except with permission from the authorities for closed auditoria or conference rooms. During day hours, the noise from loudspeakers shall not exceed the ambient standards for the area by more than 10 dB(A).

## Rule 5 — Firecrackers and Bursting of Crackers
Use of firecrackers is not permitted at night (10 PM to 6 AM). This restriction applies to all zones including during festivals unless specifically exempted by the district authority for a defined time window.

## Rule 7 — Enforcement Authority
The district magistrate or commissioner of police is the authority for complaints regarding noise from loudspeakers and public gatherings. The State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB in Karnataka) enforces industrial and vehicular noise standards. Police may seize sound equipment used in violation and file cases under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 and relevant state laws.
