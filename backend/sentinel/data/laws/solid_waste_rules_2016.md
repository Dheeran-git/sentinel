# Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016

## Rule 4 — Duties of Waste Generators
Every waste generator must segregate waste at source into at least three streams: wet (biodegradable), dry (recyclable), and domestic hazardous waste. Generators must hand over segregated waste to authorised waste collectors and must not mix streams after segregation.

## Rule 5 — Prohibition on Littering and Open Dumping
No waste generator shall throw, burn, or bury solid waste on streets, footpaths, open public land, drains, water bodies, or railway tracks. Littering in public spaces is an offence. Dumping waste on vacant plots or roadsides is explicitly prohibited.

## Rule 15 — Duties of Urban Local Bodies
The Urban Local Body (ULB) is the primary enforcement authority for household and commercial solid waste. In Bengaluru, the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) is responsible for door-to-door collection, transportation, processing, and disposal of municipal solid waste. BBMP must ensure no open dumping occurs within its jurisdiction.

## Rule 17 — Prohibition on Open Burning
Open burning of waste is prohibited in all urban areas. Burning waste in streets, open grounds, or waste collection points is an offence. BBMP officers are empowered to issue spot fines.

## Rule 24 — Enforcement and Penalties
Violation of these rules attracts action under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. The State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB for Karnataka) may also direct ULBs and generators to comply. Fines may be levied on waste generators and local bodies for non-compliance.
