# Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974

## Section 2 — Definitions: Sewage, Trade Effluent, Stream
"Sewage effluent" means effluent from sewage treatment and "trade effluent" includes any liquid waste from trade or industrial premises. "Stream" includes rivers, watercourses, inland waters, subterranean waters, sea, coastal waters, drains, and nullahs.

## Section 24 — Prohibition on Polluting Streams and Wells
No person shall knowingly cause or permit any poisonous, noxious, or polluting matter to enter any stream or well. No person shall throw any rubbish, refuse, or other matter into any stream or drain so as to obstruct flow or cause pollution. Discharge of untreated sewage or effluent into a stream, nullah, storm drain, or lake is a direct violation.

## Section 25 — Prohibition on Discharge Without Consent
No person shall establish or take steps to establish any industry, operation, or process or any treatment and disposal system that may discharge sewage or trade effluent into a stream or sewer without prior consent of the State Pollution Control Board. Consent specifies the permitted quality and quantity of discharge.

## Section 26 — Existing Discharges Require Consent
Persons who were discharging effluent into streams before the commencement of this Act are also required to obtain consent from the State Board. Discharging without consent or in violation of consent conditions is an offence.

## Section 33A — Enforcement Authority
The State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB in Karnataka) is the primary enforcement authority for water pollution. It can issue directions to close or regulate industrial units causing pollution. CPCB provides national oversight. BBMP is responsible for sewage infrastructure and preventing illegal discharge into stormwater drains in Bengaluru.

## Section 43 — Penalties
Contravention of Section 24 or 25 is punishable with imprisonment of one and a half years to six years and a fine. Continued violation after conviction attracts enhanced penalties. The Board may file complaints before designated courts.
